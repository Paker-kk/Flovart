# VOX editorial collage direction

This Flovart adaptation preserves the creative method of the MIT-licensed
[`avabbbb/vox-director`](https://github.com/avabbbb/vox-director) project while
removing its Provider, credential, polling, download, retry, and local rendering
implementation.

## Story layer

- Pick one arc: `hook_payoff`, `pas`, `bab`, `aida`, `how_it_works`, `timeline`,
  `origin`, `myth_buster`, or `listicle`.
- Make the first headline deliver a promise, question, mistake, or surprising fact
  within three seconds.
- Change the visual idea every 3–5 seconds.
- Pair a wide establishing shot with a close/detail cut-in for each beat.
- End on a payoff, a two-second CTA, or a line that closes the loop.

## Look layer

Build every keyframe as an editorial paper collage, not a smooth illustration:

- separate printed cut-outs with visible torn or scissor-cut edges;
- tape, halftone, newsprint, grain, ink misregistration, and paper shadows;
- one limited flat palette per beat;
- short, bold headline typography with a clear poster hierarchy;
- straight-on, scanned-flat composition;
- rigid 2D paper layers rather than photoreal, CGI, or soft 3D forms.

Compose the image prompt from five blocks: stable style language, separable scene
pieces, flat background palette, exact short headline, and delivery aspect/resolution.
Keep the style language consistent across shots while scene and palette progress.

## Theme bake-off

Propose three or four themes that fit the topic's era, culture, and tone. Useful
starting points include:

- `american-retro`: slab/wood type, aged halftone, bold primaries;
- `swiss-modern`: modular grid, restrained duotone, condensed grotesque;
- `punk-zine`: photocopy texture, ransom-note type, one spot color;
- `soviet-constructivist`: diagonal geometry, red/black/cream, newsprint;
- `wpa-propaganda`: muted screenprint, stencil type, public-history tone;
- `70s-groovy`: mustard/rust/avocado, display serif, riso grain;
- `chinese-ink`: woodblock/ink, vermilion seal, rice-paper texture;
- `atomic-age`: teal/orange/cream, retro-futurist forms, halftone.

Render or describe the same representative beat in each candidate. Let the user
choose by eye and persist only the approved theme in the ProductionSpec.

## Motion layer

Describe motion instead of redrawing the still:

- use one camera move per shot: `static`, `push_in`, `pull_out`, `pan`, `tilt`,
  `parallax`, or `element`;
- vary adjacent camera moves;
- animate multiple scene-appropriate paper elements while keeping them rigid;
- preserve the layout, headline, logos, textures, and dimensional lock;
- use one continuous move that settles rather than internal jump cuts;
- reserve a traveling hero element for occasional emphasis.

Generate and approve the collage keyframe first. Image-to-video should animate an
already successful poster; it should not be asked to invent the visual identity.

## Review gates

Block paid motion until the story, theme, keyframe, and OCR checks pass. Verify:

- collage depth and print texture;
- adjacent-shot rhythm and camera variation;
- headline legibility and absence of invented text;
- stable people, brands, and factual labels;
- narrator consistency, music ducking, captions, duration, aspect, codec, and
  final media verification.
